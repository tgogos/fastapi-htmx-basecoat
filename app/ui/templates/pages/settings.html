Where is the page shell?

Copy blank.html. Pages extend layout.html and replace the content and breadcrumb blocks. The Shell card stores shell_layout as header or sidebar. The View card stores content_width as wide or centered.

How is colour mode stored?

The header switcher calls Basecoat's theme API. The mode is saved in localStorage as themeMode: light or dark. The palette button stores colorTheme: default, blue, teal, or grey.
